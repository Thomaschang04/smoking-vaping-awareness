Objective & Scope

Understanding why this awareness matters

🎯

Objective

To raise awareness about the disadvantages of smoking and vaping among students and the general public by presenting evidence-based information in an accessible, engaging digital format. This project aims to encourage informed decision-making and promote a healthier lifestyle free from tobacco and vaping products.

🔭

Know the Difference

Smoking vs Vaping

Both are harmful — here's how they differ

🚬

Traditional Cigarettes

Burns tobacco to produce smoke containing nicotine, tar, carbon monoxide, and thousands of other harmful chemicals. Long-term use is strongly linked to cancer, heart disease, and lung disease.

💨

Vapes / E-Cigarettes

Heats a liquid to produce an aerosol — not harmless water vapour. May contain nicotine, fine particles, flavouring chemicals, and other substances. The long-term health effects are still being researched.

The Three Major Disadvantages

Why You Should Stay Away

Evidence-based reasons to avoid smoking and vaping

🫁 Health & Addiction
1

Damage to Health & Risk of Addiction

Cigarette smoking is a major cause of preventable disease. Tobacco smoke contains thousands of chemicals that can damage body cells. Long-term smoking increases the risk of lung cancer, heart disease, stroke, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).

Vaping is not risk-free either. Many vape liquids contain nicotine, which is highly addictive. Nicotine exposure is particularly concerning for adolescents because the brain is still developing. Vaping aerosols can also contain substances that irritate the lungs.

🇲🇾 Malaysian Example

A student starts vaping because friends say it is fashionable. If the product contains nicotine, repeated use can lead to dependence — making it difficult to concentrate on studies and spending money meant for meals or books.

💸 Financial Burden
2

Financial Burden on Individuals & Families

A seemingly small daily expense can grow into a massive annual cost. At RM15 per day, spending adds up to RM450/month or RM5,475/year. That money could go towards food, education, transport, or savings.

There are also indirect costs — medical expenses, time away from work, reduced income, and the burden on family members who must provide care.

🇲🇾 Malaysian Example

For a Malaysian household, RM450 a month could pay utility bills, buy groceries, or cover school expenses. A young person spending pocket money on vaping may have less left for lunch, books, or transport — creating unnecessary financial pressure.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Community Impact
3

Harm to Other People & the Community

Second-hand smoke contains harmful substances and affects non-smokers — especially children, the elderly, pregnant women, and people with breathing conditions. Vape aerosol is not clean air either; bystanders may inhale nicotine and other substances.

Cigarette butts and vape devices also create environmental waste. Filters don't break down easily, and discarded devices contain batteries and electronic components.

🇲🇾 Malaysian Example

In an apartment in Kuala Lumpur or Kuching, smoke from a balcony drifts into neighbouring homes. At parks and beaches, cigarette butts and vape waste litter shared spaces — affecting families, tourists, and the environment.

Key Facts

Scroll through these important facts ➜

☠️

Leading Cause of Preventable Death

Tobacco kills more than 8 million people each year, including 1.2 million non-smokers exposed to second-hand smoke.

🧪

Not Just Water Vapour

Vape aerosol can contain nicotine, fine particles, flavouring chemicals, and other potentially harmful substances. It is not harmless.

🧠

Youth Brain at Risk

Nicotine exposure is especially harmful for adolescents because the brain continues developing until around age 25.

🏥

Hidden Medical Costs

Smoking-related illnesses lead to medical expenses, time off work, reduced income, and caregiving burdens on family members.

🌍

Environmental Pollution

Cigarette filters are one of the most littered items globally. Vape devices add electronic waste with batteries and chemicals.

👶

Children Are Vulnerable

Children exposed to second-hand smoke face increased risks of respiratory infections, asthma, and ear infections.

🔗

Addiction Traps

Nicotine creates strong dependence. Users feel intense cravings and find it extremely difficult to stop once addicted.

🛡️

Make the Informed Choice

Smoking is strongly linked to serious diseases. Vaping is not harmless and can expose users to nicotine and other potentially harmful substances.

As members of society, we should make informed decisions, avoid starting these habits, support those who want to stop, and respect shared spaces.

"The consequences of smoking and vaping are not limited to the user. They can affect health, family finances, and the community as a whole."
